# 🕌 awqat - Prayer Times, Quietly in Your Bar  

## 🚀 Getting Started  

Welcome! awqat is a friendly little application that sits quietly in your computer's taskbar and shows you the times for your daily prayers. It's designed for people who want to know when to pray without fussing with complicated apps or websites. This guide will help you get it on your computer in just a couple of minutes.  

### 🧭 What Does awqat Do?  

Think of awqat as your personal prayer-time assistant that lives right in the corner of your screen. Here's what it does for you:  

- **Shows prayer times** – Displays the five daily prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha) in a small, easy-to-read panel.  
- **Finds your location automatically** – No need to type in your city or zip code. awqat figures out where you are and calculates the correct times for your area.  
- **Countdown timers** – See exactly how much time is left until the next prayer, so you never miss it.  
- **Gentle notifications** – Get a polite notification on your screen when it's time to pray.  
- **Adhan (call to prayer)** – Optionally, you can enable a beautiful adhan sound to play at prayer time.  
- **Runs in the background** – It doesn't take over your screen. It just sits there, quietly, doing its job.  

All of this comes in a lightweight package that won't slow down your computer.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions  

### What is Omachy?  
Omachy is the name of the desktop environment (the look and feel of your computer's interface) that awqat is built for. If you're using a standard Windows setup, you don't need to worry about it – awqat works fine on its own.  

### Do I need an internet connection?  
Only for the very first setup, when awqat figures out your location. After that, it works offline.  

### Will it slow down my computer?  
No. awqat is extremely lightweight. It uses very little memory and doesn't run heavy processes in the background.  

### Can I move the app to my taskbar?  
Yes! If you want awqat to always be visible, just right-click the icon in your taskbar and select "Pin to taskbar." It'll stay there for quick access.  

### Is my personal information safe?  
Absolutely. awqat only uses your location to calculate prayer times. It doesn't collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your computer.  

### What if I have a problem?  
If you ever run into trouble, you can check the "Issues" section on the awqat GitHub page. There, you can search for solutions or ask the community for help.

If you like knowing what's under the hood, here's a bit about how awqat is made:

- **Platform:** Linux (with a focus on Hyprland and Omachy environments).  
- **Special features:** Automatic location detection via GPS or network, accurate prayer-time calculations using standard astronomical formulas, and a built-in adhan player.  

You don't need to know any of this to use awqat, but it's nice to know it's solidly built.
